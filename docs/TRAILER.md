# Trailer

An opening cinematic of about 2 minutes that tells the story ([STORY.md](STORY.md)) and sets the mood before the first
match. A film trailer, not gameplay. It doesn't have to look like the game, but whatever the story names has to match
it: the ore's colour, each side's colours and emblem, and any of our buildings or units that appear (see
[FACTIONS.md](FACTIONS.md) and the model previews in `apps/game/public/assets/previews/`).

## The idea

One woman tells it as a memory. She was a child of a harvester crew under Aster when it fell, and she belongs to
neither side. Her voice runs almost the whole way, quiet and close.

The world is grey: overcast, hazy, washed out. The only warm light in it is the gold of the ore, so the eye always
goes to it. Ordan's violet and Sable's orange appear, but muted. Shots are calm and mostly still, cut every 2–4
seconds, with fades to black between the chapters. The fighting is one short burst near the end.

## Making it

- **Real photos, grey world:** stills are photographic, like frames from a film shot on location: overcast, hazy,
  desaturated, real skin and dust. The gold ore is the only warm colour.
- **People:** the narrator, her mother as a child's memory, crews and survivors are civilians. No soldiers: both
  armies are machines only.
- **Same faces:** the narrator's first approved still is the reference for all her other shots, and for her mother.
- **Stills first:** each shot starts from a still, which the user approves before any video is made.
- **Calm motion:** clips are slow push-ins and small movements; only the burst (24–29) is fast.
- **Sound:** no sound from the video model. One narrator voice and a music score from ElevenLabs, with only a few
  sound effects where they matter (the impact, the burst).
- **No generated text:** the title is set in the edit, never generated.
- **Before any paid video generation:** the prompt, the still, the model and the cost are agreed for each shot.
- **In the game:** plays once on the first launch, skippable, and can be watched again from the menu.
