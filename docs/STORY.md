# Story

The world of Gravity Harvest and why its two sides fight. The trailer script is in [TRAILER.md](TRAILER.md); how
each side looks is in [FACTIONS.md](FACTIONS.md), which wins wherever the two disagree.

## The world: Veyra

On Veyra, a golden ore grows in the ground: faceted, metallic rock that pushes against gravity. A rich field lifts
whatever stands above it; the ore once held whole cities floating above the plains, and it still powers every engine.
Harvested, it grows back, but slowly.

## The Consortium

For a hundred years one shared mining company, the Consortium, harvested the ore carefully and everyone lived off
it. Its planners lived in the floating capital, Aster; its crews dug and refined the ore in the foundry districts
below. Its machines are the common infrastructure both sides still build and use today: off-white armour, yellow
industrial markings and electric-blue energy (the Assembly Yard, Refinery, Power Plant, Motor Works, Radar, Reactor
Plant, Service Bay and the harvesters).

## The Drop

Demand outgrew what the fields could give. The planners raised the quotas, the crews drilled deeper, and one season
the fields beneath Aster were mined hollow. Their lift failed, and the capital fell out of the sky in a single night.
Where it struck, the planet itself cracked open: a molten rift, the Scar, that still glows. The game's logo is that
moment.

## The split

The survivors blamed each other, and the Consortium broke in two.

- **Ordan**, the Consortium's engineers and administrators, say the ore must be *controlled*: strict quotas, precise
  machines, order above everything. They blame the crews' greed for the Drop: the drills went deeper than any plan
  allowed. Silver-white and pale ceramic, violet energy, the bird-of-prey emblem.
- **Sable**, the crews of the deep foundries, say the ore must be *taken and used* before it runs out: compress it,
  weaponise it, survive. They blame Ordan's quotas for the Drop, and Ordan's rationing after it, which left the lower
  districts starving in the dark. Dark gunmetal and charcoal, orange ember energy, the wolf-head emblem. Sable's
  people speak with an Irish accent.

## The war

The fields that are left grow back too slowly to feed both sides, and every field mined too deep widens the Scar.
Whoever holds the harvest holds the future, if there is any future left to hold.

**Tagline:** *What holds the world together will tear it apart.*
