Controls

Left-click a unit to select it, shift to add, or drag a box to select a group.

Harvesters: click ore to harvest, a refinery to unload there. Riflemen: click an enemy (red) to attack; idle troops fire at anything in range. Click ground to move.

Build from the right panel: a ghost follows the mouse, green = OK, red = blocked. Left-click places, right-click / Esc cancels. Sell / Repair (top of the panel): click your buildings to sell them (half back; one still under construction is cancelled for all of it) or repair them (it costs as it heals); right-click or Esc to stop. The × on a unit card cancels one in production (all of it back). E with an Assembler selected deploys it into a new Assembly Yard where it stands. Carriers: with soldiers selected, click (or right-click) your Carrier and they get in, up to five; Unload (or E) with it selected lets them out. Passengers die with it. Power Plants keep the lights on; low power slows refineries and kills the radar (minimap).

Control groups: Ctrl+number (1 to 9, 0) saves the selection as that group (Cmd+number works too where the browser allows it). The number selects the group again; press it twice quickly to centre on it. Shift+number adds the group to the selection. The panel at the bottom shows what's selected: click a unit type to keep only those, shift-click to drop them.

Attack-move: with units selected, Ctrl+right-click the ground (or Ctrl+click on a Mac): they drive there but stop to fight any enemy, unit or building, they meet on the way, then carry on. Hold Ctrl to see the attack-move cursor. Harvesters just go there.

Build panel: Tab / Shift+Tab switch to the next / previous tab (Build, Units, Defense). Alt+1 to Alt+9 and Alt+0 (⌥ Option on a Mac) press the first ten cards of the tab, as a click would: queue a unit, or pick a building to place. Each card shows its key in a corner.

Goal: the enemy base (red) is in the far corner, hidden by fog of war. Scout it, build up, and destroy every enemy building. They only guard their base and won't come after you. Click an enemy building with troops selected to attack it.

Right-click deselects · right-drag / WASD pan · + / − zoom (Z resets) · ` or the gear: shortcuts and debug · Esc opens the menu (save, load, quit). The game autosaves every couple of minutes.
