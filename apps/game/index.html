Controls

Left-click a unit to select it, shift to add, or drag a box to select a group.

Harvesters: click ore to harvest, a refinery to unload there. Riflemen: click an enemy (red) to attack; idle troops fire at anything in range. Click ground to move.

Build from the right panel: a ghost follows the mouse, green = OK, red = blocked. Left-click places, right-click / Esc cancels. Sell / Repair (top of the panel): click your buildings to sell them (half back; one still under construction is cancelled for all of it) or repair them (it costs as it heals); right-click or Esc to stop. The × on a unit card cancels one in production (all of it back). E with an Assembler selected deploys it into a new Assembly Yard where it stands. Power Plants keep the lights on; low power slows refineries and kills the radar (minimap).

Goal: the enemy base (red) is in the far corner, hidden by fog of war. Scout it, build up, and destroy every enemy building. They only guard their base and won't come after you. Click an enemy building with troops selected to attack it.

Right-click deselects · right-drag / WASD pan · + / − zoom (0 resets) · ` or the gear: shortcuts and debug · Esc opens the menu (save, load, quit). The game autosaves every couple of minutes.
